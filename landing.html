Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

No fine print. Here is exactly where your data lives and what the app can and cannot do.

Read the source on GitHub
Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Every expense lives in your browser's localStorage. There is no backend, no analytics, and no upload — when you import a PDF, it is read locally.

Do I need an account?

No. The dashboard works immediately. Signup and login are a local demo and store credentials in plain text in your browser, so please do not use a real password.

Which bank statements can I import?

Text-based PDFs with debit lines shaped like DD-MM-YYYY <description> <amount>(Dr). Scanned or image-only PDFs will not work because the app does not run OCR.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

In your browser's localStorage. To erase everything, clear site data for this page in your browser settings. There is no cloud copy to delete.

Can I edit or delete an expense?

Not yet. Expenses can be added individually or imported from a statement, but editing and deleting are on the roadmap.
